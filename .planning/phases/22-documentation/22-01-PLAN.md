- stopping_condition: STOPATBEST (0) or STOPATFIRST (1)
- branching_bias: assignment bias value for branching formula
- branching_weights: per-variable weight array
- branching_factor: weight of branching_weights term in branching formula
- bias_factor: weight of assignment_bias term in branching formula
- look_ahead_factor: weight of look-ahead term in branching formula
